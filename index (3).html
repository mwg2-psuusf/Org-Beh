The six steps

Tap a step to open it.

1. Start with a diagnosis
  • Don’t leap into action
  • Assess four areas: goals, trust, engagement, outside pressures
  • Listen to employees
  • Surface what’s going unspoken
2. Rebuild psychological safety
  • Trust suffers after disruption
  • Create space for vulnerability
  • Model openness yourself
  • Invite feedback
3. Reestablish direction
  • Reground the team in its core purpose
  • Clarify goals
  • Reaffirm the team’s value to the organization
  • Set a forward-looking tone that motivates and aligns
4. Reset ways of working
  • Define roles and decision rights
  • Team norms: how we work together
  • Be explicit about expectations
  • Streamline meetings
5. Build momentum with quick wins
  • Reinforce positive behavior by making successes visible
  • Celebrate progress
  • Reflect on lessons learned
  • Renew team rituals to keep energy high
6. Stay flexible
  • A relaunch isn’t a one-time event
  • Reassess the state of the team regularly
  • Course-correct as needed to maintain momentum
